Terms & privacy notice

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Using Clippa

Clippa is a preview workspace for video clip creation. Gemini-assisted highlight selection and MP4 exports are offered for supported uploads. Signed-in users can save project titles and settings when account services are configured. Automatic captions and paid subscriptions are not currently offered. No result or performance guarantee is made.

Your content

Only use content you own or have permission to use. You retain ownership. Do not use the service for unlawful content, infringement, abuse, or attempts to access other accounts.

Accounts and saving

Guest workspace selections are temporary and cleared when you reload or leave. Signed-in users can save project titles and settings. Videos are uploaded temporarily when you request clipping and consent to Gemini analysis. Original videos and finished clips are not saved to your account. Keep your account credentials private.

Upload limits

This starter accepts videos up to 200 MiB (209,715,200 bytes, displayed as 200 MB) and between 15 seconds and 10 minutes long. It allows three valid uploads per rolling 24 hours per connection and processes one video at a time. Connections sharing an IP address may share the upload limit. Limits are held in server memory and reset on server restart.

Privacy and video processing

Uploaded videos are processed temporarily on the server. A compressed analysis copy is sent to Google Gemini. Google processes this content under its Gemini API terms, which can differ for paid and unpaid usage. Review those terms before submitting sensitive content.

Server input copies are removed when processing finishes. Temporary output clips expire after about one hour or earlier on server restart. Anyone with the exact temporary output link can access that clip until it expires. Download your clips promptly.

Supabase handles authentication and saved project settings when configured. It processes your email, account information, and authentication data. Your browser holds a temporary session token while signed in. Saved projects contain titles, settings, and dates. Hosting and authentication providers may process operational logs. This version includes no advertising or analytics scripts.

Plans

The current preview is free. Pro is a planned offering. Prices and final features have not been set. Any future charges will be shown before purchase and require agreement.

Availability and changes

The service may change or be unavailable. Keep your own copies of important work. Processing depends on available resources, supported formats, and provider availability. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the service is provided as available without warranties. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Deleting data and contacting us
