Terms & privacy notice

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Using Clippa

Clippa is a preview workspace for video clip creation. Gemini-assisted highlight selection and MP4 exports are offered for supported uploads. Automatic captions and paid subscriptions are not currently offered. No result or performance guarantee is made.

Your content

Only use content you own or have permission to use. You retain ownership. Do not use the service for unlawful content, infringement, abuse, or attempts to access other accounts.

Accounts and saving

Guest work is temporary and is cleared when you reload or leave. Signed-in users can save project titles and settings. Videos are temporarily uploaded for processing when you choose Find & create clips and consent to Gemini analysis. They are not saved to your account. Keep your account credentials private.

Privacy

Account authentication and saved settings are handled by Supabase when configured. It processes your email, account information, and authentication data. Uploaded videos are temporarily processed on the server and sent to Google Gemini. Google processes these according to its Gemini API terms, which can differ for paid and unpaid usage. Do not submit sensitive content without reviewing those terms. Server input copies are removed when processing finishes; output clips expire after about one hour or on server restart. Temporary output URLs are bearer links: anyone with the exact link can access that clip until expiration. Your browser holds a temporary session token while signed in. Clippa stores project titles, settings, and dates in your account. Hosting and authentication providers may process operational logs. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in this version.

Plans

The current preview is free. Pro is a planned offering; prices and limits have not been set. Any future charges will be shown before purchase and require agreement.

Availability and changes

The service may change or be unavailable. Keep your own copies of important work. To the extent permitted by applicable law, it is provided as available without warranties. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Deleting data and contacting us

You can delete saved projects from My projects. Account deletion and privacy requests must be directed to the operator at the contact below.
